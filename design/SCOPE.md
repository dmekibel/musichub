# MusicHub: the full scope (draft 2, 2026-10-11)

**What it is:** the whole world of music as one connected, beautiful archive that does the math.
Hear any piece more deeply, travel through music history and world music, learn theory and ears
step by step, and play with the deepest systems (mDecks-level harmony maps, Barry Harris).
Wikipedia + Spotify + Duolingo + mDecks + Radio Garden, curated, with one design language.

How this list was made: David's own invention habits (DAVID-MODEL §3) run across all of music, then
curated (P22: the best features, not the most). Tiers: **1** = version 1 can't be the product without it,
**2** = next, **3** = later. Design comes after this list, not before.

## The menus that hold it

- **Four rooms:** Today · Explore · Learn · Lab. Never a fifth tab.
- **The corner:** You (profile, saved).
- **Always available:** search, the mini-player, and one tap on any name to open its page.

## Data, honestly
- **Public-domain classical:** full playback and exact analysis from scores.
- **Recorded music:** facts from MusicBrainz and Wikidata; listening through Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube links or licensed previews.
- **Song harmony:** curated by hand (progressions aren't copyrightable, recordings are).
- **World traditions:** sourced from ethnomusicology references, every claim cited.
